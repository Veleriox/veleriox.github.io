Roads We Use: Stories We Ignore

A documentary on the choices we make on the road—and the consequences that follow.

About This Documentary

Created for the Delhi Road Safety Contest, this 4-minute documentary follows an ordinary teenager's journey through a single day on the road. Through real moments—signal violations, phone distractions, careless crossings—we explore how small decisions can have life-altering consequences.

The film doesn't lecture. Instead, it shows. It captures the disconnect between knowing the rules and choosing to ignore them. Every day, thousands of people navigate city roads. Every day, someone isn't as lucky.

  • Real-world scenarios and authentic storytelling
  • Interviews with everyday commuters and traffic experts
  • Professional cinematography and sound design
  • A wake-up call that hits close to home

Road safety isn't about rules. It's about coming home.

What You'll See

  • Signal Violations — How a few seconds can seem more important than safety
  • Phone Distractions — The illusion that "just one second" won't matter
  • Careless Crossings — When convenience becomes risk
  • Real Consequences — The moment everything changes

The Message

"Roads connect lives. Let's not turn them into risks."

Every day, you make a choice. Every day, someone else does too. When we ignore the rules, we're not just risking our own lives—we're risking theirs.

This documentary was created to remind us that road safety is everyone's responsibility. Not because of rules or laws, but because every person on the road matters.
